# Safety and scope

## What this is

An educational and research-prototyping kit. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool,
and not intended for clinical monitoring or clinical decisions.

PPG signals and every score derived from them depend heavily on sensor placement, motion,
contact pressure, ambient light, skin tone, individual physiology and calibration. Treat all
outputs as teaching artifacts.

## General rules

- Use it only for education and research prototyping.
- Do not use any output for medical diagnosis or clinical decisions.
- Keep sensor contact gentle. Too much pressure hurts and degrades the signal.
- Do not collect or publish participant data without the right consent and approvals. See
  [DATA_POLICY.md](DATA_POLICY.md).

## Extra rules for Project 04, voluntary apnea

- It is a short, voluntary, supervised activity only.
- The participant starts and ends the breath-hold, and stops at the first sign of discomfort.
- Never encourage longer holds, competition, or repeated holds.
- Stop at once on dizziness, pain, discomfort, anxiety, tingling, or shortness of breath.
- Not for anyone who is pregnant, or who has a cardiovascular, respiratory or seizure condition,
  or who is uncomfortable with the activity for any reason. Offer them the simulator scenario
  instead.
- The displayed SpO2 value is an uncalibrated educational proxy, not a measurement. Do not use it
  to reassure or alarm anyone.

## If you adapt this for a course

Follow your institution's ethics or IRB process for any human-subject data you keep or analyse
beyond a live in-class demo.
